The sustained-vowel region is the centered, longest exclusion-free reviewed stable phonation span, limited to 2 s. WSTG uses its human-confirmed trial and reviewed speech intervals. Bamboo sentence spans come from the 97 canonical words in Final Alignment Contract V1, not provider tokens, TextGrid, or MFA execution. The eight sentence lexical ranges are 1–8, 9–16, 17–30, 31–45, 46–60, 61–75, 76–91, 92–97. A sentence must have eligible reviewed speech. Features use the median of valid sentences within a trial (minimum four of eight), then median of valid trial values within a recording. Continuous intervals are transformed separately; no filter, WPD, or RQA segment crosses an exclusion, gap, sentence, or trial. Native audio is untouched; a private 48-kHz `resample_poly` copy is made for fixed-rate features. The F01 pitch contour is measured at the native rate.

## Entropy and wavelets

Entropy summarizes dispersion of a probability distribution, but PPE, TSFEL signal entropy, and WPD coefficient entropy use different distributions. Their values must not be pooled or treated as equivalent. Sample entropy lacks a frozen input representation, embedding, tolerance, distance, and aggregation definition; `vowel_sample_entropy_pending_definition` is nonselectable.

PPE uses F01 Praat autocorrelation F0 at 5 ms, floor 60 Hz, ceiling 500 Hz. Require tracking yield ≥0.80 and ≥50 voiced samples. With `f_low = mean(F0)/sqrt(2)`, set `p[m]=12 log2(F0[m]/f_low)`. An order-two covariance prediction is fit by least squares without an intercept: `p[m]≈φ1 p[m−1]+φ2 p[m−2]`. Histogram residuals `r[m]` into 31 equal bins on [−1.5,+1.5] semitones, excluding out-of-range residuals; require ≥20 retained residuals. PPE is `−Σ p_i log2(p_i)` bits and is at most `log2(31)`. Unreliable contour and numerical failures yield NaN with explicit reasons. Exact IRAPT parity is not claimed.

The WPD backbone is PyWavelets WaveletPacket `db4`, level 3, `symmetric` extension, terminal nodes in frequency order. Each continuous eligible piece is decomposed independently. Node energy is `E_j=Σ_n c_j[n]^2`; C075 exposes `E_j/Σ_k E_k`. C074 exposes `H_j/log2(N_j)` where `p_j[n]=c_j[n]^2/E_j` and `H_j=−Σ_n p_j[n] log2(p_j[n])`. Raw energy and entropy bits are audit values. Daubechies order and coefficient normalization are VSLP choices because the reviewed source does not freeze them. If PyWavelets is unavailable, the affected leaves return `pywt_unavailable`.

## MFCC recurrence components

The MFCC frontend reuses F12's 48-kHz, 25-ms Hann, 10-ms hop, 2048-point FFT, 40 mel filters, orthonormal DCT-II definition. `mfcc01=C0`, ..., `mfcc13=C12`. C076 measures ordinary MFCC trajectories. C077 applies a five-frame regression delta at the 10-ms frame rate with replicated edges, then measures delta trajectories. Each uninterrupted 1-s segment is independently standardized by its sample SD. RQA uses m=3, τ=5 frames, Euclidean distance, ε=0.2 standardized units, identity diagonal excluded, and diagonal-line minimum length 2. `DET=recurrence points in qualifying diagonal lines / all recurrence points`, then median across valid segments. Higher DET indicates more determinism and generally *less* dynamical complexity. No coefficient is silently assumed to be the source's ambiguously numbered “MFCC1.”

## Rhythm and regularity

The VSLP filterbank has 28 bands with 29 edges equally spaced on Greenwood cochlear position from 100 to 10,000 Hz: `x=log10(f/165.4+1)/2.1`, `f=165.4(10^(2.1x)−1)`. Each band uses fourth-order zero-phase Butterworth SOS bandpass. Hilbert-magnitude envelopes are resampled to 100 Hz with `scipy.signal.resample_poly`, smoothed with a second-order zero-phase 40-Hz Butterworth low-pass, and trimmed 100 ms at both ends. Critical bands are 100–300, 300–800, 1000–3000, and 3000–8000 Hz, with arithmetic mean of contributing narrowband envelopes. The exact 29 edges and filter design are audit metadata. Historical filter coefficients are unavailable, so exact source-filterbank parity is pending.

C078 computes Hamming-windowed 2048-point one-sided modulation *power*, excluding DC and retaining Nyquist. Each modulation depth is band power divided by total positive-frequency modulation power. Bands are delta 0.9–2.5 Hz, theta 2.5–12 Hz, beta/gamma 12–40 Hz. These bands indicate slower versus faster envelope modulation, not a clinical diagnosis. C079 bandpasses processed critical-band envelopes with fourth-order zero-phase Butterworth filters, uses Hilbert phase, and measures `|mean(exp(i(2φ_delta−φ_theta)))|` or `|mean(exp(i(3φ_theta−φ_beta_gamma)))|`; higher values indicate stronger harmonic phase coupling. Its visibility leaf takes sample SD (`ddof=1`) of complete nonoverlapping 10-ms speech windows, concatenates resulting SD values only after per-interval measurement, and computes natural-visibility density `2m/[M(M−1)]`. This differs from F11's 50-ms visibility construct.

The historical healthy-control articulation-rate time normalization is deferred to downstream Feature Analysis until an external, pre-frozen normative reference exists. The current analysis cohort is never used to derive that reference. F08 Bamboo articulation rate is support for future factor research, not a duplicate F13 leaf. Factor scores remain unavailable because archived scaling, loadings, and scoring intercepts are absent. Nonlinear components can remain sensitive to microphone response, codec, AGC, noise, bandwidth, and clipping; no Q regression is performed during extraction.

TSFEL 0.2.0 `tsfel.entropy(signal, prob="standard")` counts **exactly equal sample values** with `np.unique`, then computes base-two Shannon entropy divided by `log2(len(signal))`. It returns 0 for a constant sequence and approximately 1 for continuous random floating-point samples whose values are nearly all unique. It is bounded nominally by 0–1. This exact-value behavior is a material interpretation limit for speech audio; no replacement histogram or quantization is substituted under the existing public IDs.

[Rong and Heidrick (2026)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1794169/full) specifies eight WPD Shannon entropy representations from third-order Daubechies WPD; it does not identify db4, symmetric extension, the VSLP coefficient-energy probability rule, or duration normalization. It directly describes delta-MFCC RQA for 1-s stationary segments with m=3, τ=5 and ε=0.2. Per-segment sample-z standardization, identity-diagonal exclusion, `l_min=2`, and median aggregation are VSLP choices. C076 ordinary-MFCC RQA is retained from the earlier C076 source audit and is **not** attributed to that 2026 delta-MFCC procedure. Its independent bibliographic parity remains unresolved. F13 reuses F12's custom MFCC frontend rather than calling `librosa.feature.mfcc`; its custom five-frame delta agrees numerically with `librosa.feature.delta(..., width=5, order=1, mode="nearest")` on a synthetic coefficient track.

The 2026 source supports 28 cochlear-position bands over 100–10,000 Hz, Hilbert envelopes, 100-Hz resampling, second-order zero-lag 40-Hz smoothing, the four stated critical bands, a Hamming-windowed 2048-point FFT, the three modulation bands, fourth-order zero-lag phase bandpasses, and empirical 2:1 and 3:1 coupling. The source does not specify exact cochlear filter coefficients, how short sentence envelopes are padded, or whether DC enters “total spectral power.” VSLP windows the **whole available envelope**, zero-pads it to 2048 samples when shorter, and uses NumPy's one-sided real FFT. Zero padding refines the sampled frequency grid and adds no temporal information. Envelopes longer than 2048 samples fail explicitly with `modulation_sequence_exceeds_fft_support`, avoiding silent FFT truncation. Modulation-depth denominator is power in **all positive-frequency bins through 50-Hz Nyquist**, excluding DC; it is not restricted to 0.9–40 Hz. These are VSLP choices, so exact numerical source parity is pending.

For 2:1 coupling, `2φ_delta−φ_theta` stays approximately constant when theta oscillates twice per delta cycle; for 3:1, `3φ_theta−φ_beta_gamma` stays approximately constant. Synthetic correct-versus-wrong harmonic tests verify the intended orientation. VSLP filters each continuous reviewed interval separately. Its 10-ms local sample-SD natural-visibility path is distinct from F11 C063's 50-ms windows. Bamboo uses each canonical sentence first, then medians across at least four valid sentences, trials, and recording; this minimum and aggregation are VSLP policy, not stated source parity. Source-rate-normalized rhythm parity remains pending because extraction does not estimate an HC reference from the current cohort.
